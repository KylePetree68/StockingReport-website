Park Alerts — Heron Lake State Park

  • All boat ramps (and courtesy docks) at Heron Lake State Park are currently unavailable for boaters due to extremely low lake levels. These ramps and docks will remain unavailable until lake levels improve. Those visitors planning to launch watercraft should call the park office to check for conditions prior to arriving. (575)588-7470 (5/26/2026 – ongoing)
  • Heron Lake is closed to the use of operation of all Off Highway Vehicles (OHV's). This includes but is not limited to Off Highway Motorcycles, Side-By Sides, Quad Runners, and Snowmobiles. (7/27/2021 – ongoing)
1 more park alert (campgrounds, trails, seasonal closures)
  • Rio Chama Trailhead and access to the Rio Chama Trail is closed due to construction in the area. Access to the Rio Chama River can be gained at the North El Vado Day Use Area. (12/20/2021 – ongoing)

Source: NM State Parks alerts, checked 2026-09-26. Park office (575) 588-7470.
