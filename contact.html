Common Reasons to Contact Us

🗺️ Location Errors

Found a water body with an incorrect map location? Let us know the correct coordinates and we'll update it.

📊 Data Issues

Noticed missing or incorrect stocking data? Send us the details and we'll investigate.

💡 Feature Requests

Have an idea for a new feature? We're always looking to improve the site!

🤝 Partnerships

Interested in partnering with us? Local guides, tackle shops, and tourism organizations welcome!

What to Include

To help us address your concern quickly, please include:

  • Water Body Name: The specific lake, river, or pond you're referring to
  • Issue Description: Clear details about what's wrong or what you'd like to see
  • Screenshots (if applicable): Images help us understand visual issues
  • Your Contact Info: So we can follow up with you if needed

Response Time

We typically respond within 2-3 business days. During busy fishing seasons or after major site updates, it may take a bit longer. Thank you for your patience!

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, StockingReport.com earns from qualifying purchases.

The “Shop tackle” button goes to an Amazon search. If you buy something after clicking it, Amazon pays this site a small commission. It costs you nothing extra and the price you pay is the same.

That commission is the only way this site earns money. It helps cover hosting and the time spent keeping the stocking data, regulations and lake levels current. Stocking data itself always comes straight from the NM Department of Game & Fish and is never influenced by affiliate revenue.
