Consumption Advisory

Managed by: NM State Parks · Clayton Lake State Park & Dinosaur Trackways Official site ↗

Open noon March 1 to noon Oct 31.

Park Alerts — Clayton Lake & Dinosaur Trackways State Park

  • Clayton Lake State Park and Dinosaur Trackway has closed access to the boat ramp and is prohibiting the use of motorized boats due to declining water levels and related safety concerns. The lake remains open for non-motorized vessels, including canoes and kayaks. Motorized boat access will be restored once water levels improve and conditions allow for safe use of the boat ramp. (7/31/2026 – ongoing)
1 more park alert (campgrounds, trails, seasonal closures)
  • Due to ongoing construction to replace vault toilets at Clayton Lake State Park & Dinosaur Trackways, the vault toilet facilities located at the dinosaur parking lot, Camp Cove, the South Loop, and the Rock Gardens will be closed. The comfort station, as well as the double vault toilets at North Point and along the Nature Trail, will remain open for visitor use. Please exercise caution and be aware of construction activity while visiting the park. (7/31/2026 – ongoing)

Source: NM State Parks alerts, checked 2026-09-20. Park office 575-374-8808.
