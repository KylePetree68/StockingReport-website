Cochiti Lake

Not on the NMDGF stocking schedule

Cochiti Lake does not appear in any NMDGF stocking report in this site's records, which run from 2021 to the present. Its fishery is self-sustaining: the species listed in the fishing rules booklet reproduce in the lake and the Rio Grande above it, so there are no stocking dates to report.

The lake is a Corps of Engineers flood-control reservoir on Pueblo de Cochiti land. It is included here because it is one of the most-fished waters in the state and because the Corps publishes a daily lake level (charted below). If another agency or group stocks Cochiti we have not found a public record of it.

Consumption Advisory

Managed by: US Army Corps of Engineers · Cochiti Lake (on Pueblo de Cochiti land; recreation areas run by the Corps) Official site ↗

Flood-control reservoir operated by the Corps under lease from Pueblo de Cochiti. Cochiti and Tetilla Peak recreation areas are Corps-run; the spillway is closed to fishing and the pueblo's shoreline outside the recreation areas is closed without tribal permission.
